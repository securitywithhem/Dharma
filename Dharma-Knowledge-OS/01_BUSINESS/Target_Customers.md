# Target Customers

From PRD Section 3 ("Target Users"):

1. **Founders & CTOs of Indian startups** — need SOC 2 / ISO 27001 fast to close enterprise deals; own company risk without dedicated security staff; want cost-effective, self-deployable tooling.
2. **Security & Compliance Officers** — track control status across frameworks, draft/review policies, maintain audit readiness.
3. **External Auditors & Evaluators** — need read-only evidence/policy-version access and proof of audit-log integrity, served via the auditor portal (see [[Authentication]], [[User_Journeys]] Journey 5).

The live product's expansion into MSSP (`MsspGrant`) and enterprise/white-label (`OrganizationSettings`, SSO) implies at least two more customer segments in practice — MSSPs managing multiple client orgs, and larger enterprises needing SSO/branding — but no persona documentation exists for these. See [[Dharma_Master_Context]].
