# Mission

> Self-hosted compliance made simple. Automate DPDP, ISO 27001, and SOC 2 locally with AI. (PRD tagline)

Concretely, Dharma exists to let a founder or compliance officer at an under-resourced organization:

1. Stand up a working compliance program (framework tracking, evidence, policies, audit trail) in hours, not the weeks a consultant-led engagement takes.
2. Do it without paying enterprise SaaS pricing or trusting a third party with sensitive data — see [[Problem_Statement]] for the specific harms this avoids.
3. Produce audit artifacts (verified evidence, published policies, a cryptographically verifiable log) that satisfy an external auditor via the [[Audit_Process]] and time-limited auditor portal.

This mission has since expanded in practice — the live product also serves MSSPs managing multiple client orgs and enterprises needing SSO/white-label — but the core promise (self-hosted, AI-assisted, auditable) is unchanged. See [[Dharma_Master_Context]] for the full current module list.
